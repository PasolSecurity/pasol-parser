# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, respectful feedback, accepting responsibility, and focusing on what is best for the community. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, sexualized attention, trolling, insults, threats, publishing private information, or other conduct inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository security contact. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently ban participants for behavior they deem inappropriate. Enforcement decisions will be explained when appropriate and handled with respect for reporter privacy.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
