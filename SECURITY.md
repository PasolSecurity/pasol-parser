# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please do not open a public issue for a suspected vulnerability. Use GitHub's private **Report a vulnerability** feature for this repository. Include the affected version, a minimal harmless reproducer if possible, impact, and suggested mitigation. Do not attach malware or sensitive files.

Maintainers will acknowledge a report within five business days and coordinate remediation and disclosure. Supported releases are the latest tagged release and the `main` branch.

Parser crashes, hangs, excessive resource use, boundary-check failures, and misleading success responses for structurally invalid inputs are security-relevant.

## Scope and safe research

Good-faith research that avoids privacy violations, service disruption, and access to data belonging to others is welcome. Test only systems and files you own or are authorized to inspect.
